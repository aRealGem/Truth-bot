The following are excerpts from President Trump's 2026 State of the Union address to Congress, delivered March 4, 2025.

And last year, the murder rate saw its single largest decline in recorded history. This is the biggest decline, think of it, in recorded history. The lowest number in over 125 years — year 1900.

The Biden administration and its allies in Congress gave us the worst inflation in the history of our country. But in 12 months, my administration has driven core inflation down to the lowest level in more than five years. And in the last three months of 2025, it was down to 1.7 percent.

In 12 months, I secured commitments for more than $18 trillion, pouring in from all over the globe. Much less than $1 trillion for four years versus much more than $18 trillion for one year.

One of the primary reasons for our country's stunning economic turnaround — the biggest in history, where the Dow Jones broke 50,000, four years ahead of schedule, and the S&P hit 7,000, where it wasn't supposed to do it for many years.

The price of eggs is down 60 percent. The cost of chicken, butter, fruit, hotels, automobiles, rent is lower today than when I took office, by a lot.
