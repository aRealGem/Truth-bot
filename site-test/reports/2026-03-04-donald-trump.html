Trump's two-part claim is partly supportable but substantially misleading. The first part—that core inflation fell to its lowest level in more than five years—has some support: CNBC reported that core CPI was up 2.5% year-over-year in January 2026, described as the lowest level since April 2021, which is roughly five years.
