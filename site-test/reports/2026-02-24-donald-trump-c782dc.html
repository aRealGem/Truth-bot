Trump's claim that core inflation was 1.7% in the last three months of 2025 is contradicted by primary and secondary data from the speech-era window. According to BEA data reported on Feb 20, 2026, core PCE — the Fed's preferred gauge — accelerated to 3.0% year-over-year in December 2025 (up from 2.8% in November), and on a Q4/Q4 basis core PCE rose 2.9% in Q4 2025. Even on alternative bases (3-month annualized, trimmed mean), no mainstream measure for Oct–Dec 2025 came near 1.7%; core PCE MoM in December 2025 was 0.4% (roughly 4.8% annualized). The 1.7% figure does not match any standard core inflation reading for Q4 2025.
