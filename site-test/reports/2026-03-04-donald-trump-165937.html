anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 ⚠️ Misleading

The numerical component of Trump's claim is directionally accurate: since the start of Fiscal Year (FY) 2021, Customs and Border Protection (CBP) has recorded more than 10.8 million encounters nationwide, including more than 8.72 million at the Southwest border, with annual totals of roughly 1,956,519 in FY2021, 2,766,582 in FY2022, 3,201,144 in FY2023, and 2,901,142 in FY2024. However, the 'totally unvetted and unchecked' portion is false. Encounter totals include a mix of Title 8 enforcement actions, Title 42 expulsions, arrests, inadmissible encounters at ports of entry, and other administrative actions, and are not equivalent to a one-to-one count of unique people who crossed the U.S.–Mexico border without authorization. FactCheck.org directly rebutted Trump's framing, noting that the idea that migrants are 'coming in totally unchecked, unvetted' is wrong, since those apprehended trying to cross illegally are interviewed, have criminal record checks and provide biometric data. Additionally, a substantial share of those encountered were expelled or removed — a majority of all southwest border encounters during the past three fiscal years resulted in a removal, return, or expulsion. So while millions were encountered, they were not 'unvetted and unchecked,' and raw encounter totals overstate unique crossings due to repeat encounters and expulsions.
