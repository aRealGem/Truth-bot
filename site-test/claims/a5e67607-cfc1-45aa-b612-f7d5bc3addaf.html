Model reasoning — Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7

DHS stated in September 2025 that 'since President Trump took office, fentanyl trafficking at the southern border is down by 56% compared to the same period in 2024,' which is the figure Trump cited. WOLA's analysis of CBP fiscal year data found a 46% drop in fentanyl seizures from FY2024 to FY2025 (11,486 pounds in FY2025). The 56% figure is based on a different time window (post-January 21, 2025 vs. same period in 2024) and refers to seizures, which are an imperfect proxy for actual flow. Whether this is 'a record' decline is also debatable — fentanyl seizures have been dropping since spring 2023, and the American Immigration Council notes seizures peaked in April 2023 and have fallen nearly every month since. The number is directionally accurate and traceable to a DHS press release, but the framing as a 'record' decline driven solely by Trump's policies oversimplifies a multi-year trend.
