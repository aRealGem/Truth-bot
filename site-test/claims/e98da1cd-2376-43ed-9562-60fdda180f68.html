anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — ❌ False High

Trump's claim that the Biden administration produced 'the worst inflation in the history of the United States' is contradicted by primary and historical data. Inflation under Biden peaked at 9.1% in June 2022, which was the highest since 1981 but far below numerous historical peaks. According to historical CPI data, the highest inflation rate in U.S. history was 23.7% in June 1920, with inflation reaching 17.84% in 1917 and 20.1% in March 1947. Federal Reserve History documents that inflation peaked near 15% in March 1980 during the Great Inflation era — also higher than the Biden-era peak. Fact-checkers at FactCheck.org, PolitiFact, and CNN have all explicitly flagged this Trump claim as false. BLS data confirms that by the end of the Biden administration (Dec 2024), CPI was running at 2.9%, and inflation in the 2021-2022 surge, while the worst in four decades, was nowhere near historical highs.
