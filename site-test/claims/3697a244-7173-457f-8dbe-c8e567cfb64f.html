Model reasoning — Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7

The claim that 'zero illegal aliens have been admitted' for nine months refers to CBP's metric of 'zero releases' by Border Patrol, not zero entries. CBP states for the seventh consecutive month, U.S. Border Patrol released zero illegal aliens into the United States. Every individual apprehended was processed according to law, and by January 2026 it was the 8th straight month of zero releases. However, Border Patrol still apprehended thousands at the border (6,478 USBP apprehensions on the southwest border in December), and CBP acknowledges that 'gotaways' (those evading apprehension) still occur. So while no apprehended migrants were released into the U.S., illegal entries (and successful evasions) have not literally been zero. The claim conflates 'zero releases' with 'zero admissions/entries.'

So the nine-month 'zero admitted' statement overstates and conflates different legal concepts.

A March 20, 2026, CBP release confirmed the policy was "zero illegal aliens released at the border," which is different from zero entering or being admitted.
