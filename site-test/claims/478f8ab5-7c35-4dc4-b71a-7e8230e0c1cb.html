anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — ❌ False High

Trump's claim that gasoline was below $2.30 a gallon in most U.S. states at the time of his March 4, 2026 State of the Union address is contradicted by both government and industry data. According to AAA data cited by multiple fact-checkers, as of Feb. 24, there were no U.S. states where the average price of a gallon of regular gasoline was below $2.30, and Oklahoma was the closest at $2.37. Looking at statewide averages, not one state had an average below $2.30 per gallon according to AAA, and no state had an average gas price below $2.37 per gallon on the day of the speech. This is further corroborated by EIA data showing that the U.S. retail price for regular grade gasoline averaged $3.10 per gallon in 2025, $0.21/gal less than in 2024, marking the third consecutive year of declining nominal retail gasoline prices, and retail gasoline prices decreased to an annual low of $2.81/gal in late December 2025—still well above $2.30. Additionally, the annual average retail price for regular grade gasoline in 2025 ranged from a low of $2.39/gal on the Gulf Coast to a high of $4.32/gal on the West Coast, meaning even the cheapest regional average was above $2.30. Trump may have been referencing individual stations rather than state averages, but his claim as stated is false.
