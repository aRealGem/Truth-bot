anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 ✅ True

The claim that gasoline peaked at over $6 per gallon in some states under President Biden is accurate. According to EIA data, the U.S. average retail price for regular-grade gasoline averaged $3.95 per gallon in 2022 and hit its 2022 high at $5.01/gal in June 2022. At the state level, California set a new record in May 2022 with an average per-gallon cost of $6.05, though the price climbed as high as $7 in remote Mono County, according to AAA. PolitiFact similarly confirms that when Biden was inaugurated in January 2021, the nationwide average price of gasoline was about $2.46 per gallon, and gasoline prices did exceed $5, but that peak came more than a year later in June 2022. So Trump's specific claim that some states saw prices top $6/gallon under Biden is factually correct — California's statewide average exceeded $6, and other high-cost states (Hawaii, Nevada, Washington, Oregon, Alaska) also saw averages above $5-6 during summer 2022.

gemini / gemini-2.5-pro ⚠️ Misleading

The claim is a mix of an accurate statement and a false one. The first part is true: gasoline prices did reach a peak of over $6 a gallon in some states under President Biden. Data from the American Automobile Association (AAA) shows that the statewide average for a gallon of regular unleaded gasoline in California surpassed $6, with the peak being $6.438 on June 14, 2022. Other states also saw prices over $5 a gallon during that period when the national average peaked at $5.016. The second part of the claim, however, is false. Donald Trump made this statement on February 24, 2026. On that day, data from AAA and news reports show that no state had an average gas price below $2.30 per gallon. Data from February 23, 2026, showed only Oklahoma was close at $2.34 per gallon, with the vast majority of states being significantly higher. Therefore, the statement that gas was "below $2.30 a gallon in most states" at the time of his speech is incorrect.
