anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — 🟢 Mostly True High

Trump's claim refers to 2025, the year before his 2026 State of the Union. Preliminary data strongly supports that 2025 saw a historic, likely record-breaking drop in murders. FBI preliminary data showed homicides fell roughly 18% between September 2024 and August 2025, and crime analyst Jeff Asher (AH Datalytics) projects a ~20% drop for 2025, which he characterizes as by far the largest decline ever recorded, eclipsing the 15% drop in 2024. The Council on Criminal Justice found a 21% decline in homicides across 35 large cities from 2024 to 2025 and projected that if nationwide FBI data aligns, it would mark the largest single-year percentage drop in the homicide rate on record, bringing the rate to about 4.0 per 100,000. CDC homicide data going back to 1900 shows the 2025 decline as the largest ever at 15%, supplanting a 14% drop in 2002. However, the claim carries important caveats: official FBI nationwide 2025 estimates won't be released until fall 2026, so the 'record' status is based on preliminary/projected data and is subject to revision. Additionally, the downward trend began in 2022-2023, well before Trump took office, so crediting his administration is misleading context. The underlying factual claim about the magnitude of the 2025 decline, however, is well-supported.
