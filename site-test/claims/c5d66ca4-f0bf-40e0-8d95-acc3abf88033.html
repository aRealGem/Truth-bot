Trump's claim that he inherited inflation 'at record levels' is false. According to the BLS, the CPI increased 3.0% for the 12 months ending January 2025, when Trump took office—well below the pandemic-era peak of 9.1% in June 2022 under Biden, and far below historical records. The BLS itself noted that the June 2022 9.1% spike was the largest 12-month increase in 40 years, but not a record; the worst U.S. inflation occurred after WWI (23.7% from June 1919 to June 1920) and in 1946 (over 18%), with sustained high rates of 12-15% in the 1970s-early 1980s. Inflation had actually been cooling for over two years before Trump took office. FactCheck.org, CNN, NPR, and AP all explicitly flagged this specific claim from Trump's March 2025 joint address as false.
