Model reasoning — Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7

CBP and DHS primary sources confirm that for seven consecutive months as of November 2025, U.S. Border Patrol released zero illegal aliens into the United States. CBP's December 4, 2025 release explicitly states 'For the seventh consecutive month, U.S. Border Patrol released zero illegal aliens into the United States.' However, this technically refers only to releases by Border Patrol after apprehension — illegal aliens are still being apprehended (over 117,000 enforcement encounters since January 21 through November on the southwest border) and processed under law, not 'admitted.' The streak of zero releases covers roughly seven months, not the full nine months claimed, though the broader policy of catch-and-release ended in January 2025, so 'admitted' as a term is largely supported.
