anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 ✅ True

Trump's claim that gasoline peaked over $6/gallon in some U.S. states under Biden is supported by both primary government data and secondary reporting. According to the EIA (Tier 1), the U.S. average retail gasoline price hit its 2022 high at $5.01/gal in June 2022, the highest gasoline price since 2012 when adjusted for inflation. State-level averages exceeded this: AAA data reported by Snopes showed California's statewide average reached about $6.40 per gallon in June 2022, well above $6. Even earlier, EIA reported California's retail gasoline price hit $5.59/gal on March 14, 2022, and continued climbing through the summer. Individual stations in California and other West Coast states posted prices well above $6 during this period. PolitiFact also confirmed gasoline prices exceeded $5 nationally with the peak in June 2022. Thus Trump's statement that prices exceeded $6 in some states during his predecessor's tenure is factually accurate.
