anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — ⚠️ Misleading High

Trump's claim that fentanyl 'flow' across the border dropped 56% conflates seizures with flow, which are not equivalent. DHS has cited a 56% figure comparing fentanyl trafficking at the southern border since Trump took office to the same period in 2024 (per a September 2025 DHS release). However, independent analysis of CBP's own seizure data shows fentanyl seizures fell roughly 48-50% from fiscal 2024 to fiscal 2025 — close to but not exactly 56%. More importantly, drug policy experts emphasize that seizure totals do not measure actual flow: the federal government cannot accurately measure how much illicit fentanyl enters the country because black markets are inherently difficult to track. A drop in seizures could reflect shifts in trafficking routes, changes in smuggling tactics (e.g., shift from pills to more concealable powder), reduced demand, or less effective enforcement — not necessarily reduced supply. Additionally, the downward trend in seizures began before Trump's tariffs took effect, undermining causal claims. The 56% figure originates from the administration itself and refers specifically to southern-border fentanyl trafficking/seizures during a specific comparison window, not overall 'flow.'
