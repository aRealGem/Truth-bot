The broad thrust is supported by federal crime data, but the wording needs care. The FBI reported on September 23, 2024, that murder and non-negligent manslaughter fell 11.6% in 2023 versus 2022, and on August 5, 2025, that it fell another 14.9% in 2024 versus 2023. That 14.9% drop in 2024 is larger than the 11.6% decline in 2023 and is consistent with descriptions of a record modern-era decline. However, the claim says “murder rate” and “single largest decline in recorded history”; the FBI release is about estimated murder counts/offenses, while historical “recorded history” can depend on dataset and methodology. CDC/NCHS has separately noted that 2020’s jump was the biggest one-year increase in over a century, underscoring that very long-run comparisons are tricky. So the claim is directionally right but slightly overbroad in its wording.
