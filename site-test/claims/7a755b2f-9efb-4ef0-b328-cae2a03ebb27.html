anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 ❌ False

Trump's claim that gasoline is as low as $1.99/gallon in some U.S. states is contradicted by both primary and secondary sources. According to the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the U.S. retail price for regular grade gasoline averaged $3.10 per gallon in 2025, and prices had been declining for three consecutive years, with the annual average retail price for regular grade gasoline in 2025 ranging from a low of $2.39/gal on the Gulf Coast to a high of $4.32/gal on the West Coast. The EIA's February 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook forecasts U.S. retail gasoline prices to average $3.70/gal in 2026 and $3.46/gal in 2027, both up from $3.10/gal in 2025. No state had a statewide average near $1.99: on the date of one of Trump's earlier $1.99 claims, no state had an average gas price below $2 per gallon according to AAA data; the lowest average in any state was about $2.31 per gallon, in Oklahoma. Around the time of Trump's March 2026 State of the Union claim, the national average for a gallon of gas reached $2.98, and the lowest average price was recorded in Oklahoma at $2.43 per gallon, far from Trump's $1.99 claim. GasBuddy's analysis found only 8 out of roughly 150,000 gas stations nationwide were selling gasoline below $2 per gallon — a tiny handful of individual stations, not entire states. As Media Bias/Fact Check summarized, only a handful of individual gas stations—not entire states—briefly offered fuel below $1.99, while statewide averages remained well above $2.35, with the national average at $3.07.
