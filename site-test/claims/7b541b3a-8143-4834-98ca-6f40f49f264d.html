anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — ✅ True High

Trump's claim that gasoline reached over $6 a gallon in some states under Biden is accurate. According to U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) data, the average U.S. retail price for regular-grade gasoline averaged $3.95 per gallon in 2022 and hit its 2022 high at $5.01/gal in June 2022. At the state level, California clearly exceeded $6/gallon: California set a record with an average per-gallon cost of $6.05, though the price climbed as high as $7 in remote Mono County, according to AAA. Secondary sources corroborate this, with statewide averages in a handful of states surpassing $5.40, with California tallying the highest at about $6.40 per gallon, per AAA data. PolitiFact confirms the broader context: when Biden was inaugurated in January 2021, the nationwide average gasoline price was about $2.46/gallon, and gasoline prices did exceed $5, but that peak came more than a year later in June 2022. Note that while the claim is accurate, presidents have limited direct control over gasoline prices, which were driven largely by post-pandemic demand recovery and Russia's invasion of Ukraine.

gemini / gemini-2.5-pro — ✅ True High

The claim is accurate. During a significant spike in gasoline prices in mid-2022 under the Biden administration, the average price for a gallon of regular gasoline exceeded $6 in several states. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, a primary government source, reported that gasoline prices averaged $6.556 a gallon in the San Francisco-Oakland-Hayward area in June 2022. Data from AAA confirms this, showing the statewide average price in California reached a peak of $6.438 on June 14, 2022. During this same period, the national average price for gasoline peaked at $5.016.
