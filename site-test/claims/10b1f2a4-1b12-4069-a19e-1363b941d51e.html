anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 — ❌ False High

Trump's March 4, 2025 joint address claim that inflation was at 'record levels' when he last addressed Congress is not supported by the data. Taken literally as '12 months ago' (March 2024), BLS data show CPI rose only about 3.5% year-over-year — far from any record. Even at its Biden-era peak, the annualized inflation rate reached 9.1% in June 2022, which was the highest 12-month increase since November 1981, but that still fell short of historical records; the worst inflation period occurred after World War I (roughly 20%+ in 1920). By the time Trump took office, the annualized rate of inflation was 3%, based on the Consumer Price Index. Fact-checkers rated the claim false, with the NYTimes noting inflation has slowed under Mr. Trump, but not by the drastic margins he is claiming, and FactCheck.org noting inflation was never the 'worst' under Biden, and it was 3% for the 12 months ending in January. BLS historical tables confirm no record-level inflation in either March 2024 or during Biden's term.
