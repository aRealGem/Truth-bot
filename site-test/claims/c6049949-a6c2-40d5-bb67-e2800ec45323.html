xai / grok-4 — ⚠️ Misleading High

The claim refers to core inflation, typically measured by core CPI or PCE, dropping to 1.7% in the last three months of 2025. Official BLS data for core CPI in October and November 2025 is unavailable due to a government shutdown, with December at 2.6% year-over-year. Trump's calculation appears to average November and December rates (2.6% each) and divide by three, assuming zero for October, yielding 1.7%—a non-standard and misleading method. BEA data shows core PCE monthly increases of 0.2%, 0.2%, and 0.4% for October, November, and December, annualizing to around 3%, contradicting the low figure. Fact-checks from sources like The Hill, CBS News, and FactCheck.org confirm this as misleading or false.
