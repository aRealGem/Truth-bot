At the time of Trump's 2026 State of the Union address (which was actually delivered February 24-25, 2026, not March 4), mortgage rates were indeed falling. Since peaking in May 2025, average 30-year mortgage rates had headed downward, and rates fell below 6% at the end of February 2026.
