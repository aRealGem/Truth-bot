Trump's claim conflates immigration terminology. In legal immigration usage, 'admitted' means lawful entry—so by definition, no one entering illegally is ever 'admitted.' The statement appears to reference a separate metric: CBP's report that U.S. Border Patrol released zero migrants on parole along the southwest border for nine consecutive months (May 2025–January 2026). CBP confirms this 'zero releases' streak (e.g., Secretary Noem cited the 7th straight month of zero releases in November 2025, and CBP reported Border Patrol released 0 illegal aliens in May 2025 versus 62,000+ in May 2024). However, the claim is misleading because (1) thousands of migrants are still encountered crossing illegally each month—CBP apprehended roughly 6,000 in January 2026 and nearly 10,000 encounters occurred at the southern border that month; (2) 'zero releases' refers specifically to parole releases by Border Patrol, not total illegal entries; (3) migrants transferred to ICE custody may still be released, and 'got-aways' who evade capture are not counted; and (4) Trump's use of 'admitted' rather than 'released' further distorts the underlying statistic. Fact-checkers at ABC News, PBS, Al Jazeera, CBS, Snopes, and CEDMO uniformly rated the literal claim as false or misleading while acknowledging that illegal crossings have dropped to historic lows.
