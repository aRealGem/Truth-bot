The claim attributed to Trump—that the 250th anniversary of American independence would occur less than five months from March 4, 2025 (i.e., July 4, 2025)—is factually incorrect. The United States declared independence on July 4, 1776, making July 4, 2025 the 249th anniversary, not the 250th. Multiple authoritative sources, including the official America250 Commission and the White House itself, confirm that on July 4, 2026, the nation will commemorate and celebrate the 250th anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Independence. The White House's own Freedom 250 page states that on July 4, 2026, America will celebrate the most important milestone in our country's history—250 years of American Independence, and Wikipedia confirms festivities will mark various events leading up to the Declaration's anniversary on the 250th Independence Day: July 4, 2026. From March 4, 2025, the actual 250th anniversary is approximately 16 months away, not less than five months. Trump's statement on March 4, 2025 conflated the timing; the correct 'less than five months' framing would have applied roughly a year later.
