anthropic / claude-opus-4-7 ❌ False

Trump's claim that gasoline is below $2.30 a gallon in most U.S. states is contradicted by both primary government data and multiple fact-checks. Gasoline prices averaged $2.92 nationwide the week of Feb. 16, 2026, down from $3.11 when Trump was inaugurated. While gas prices had dropped from a national peak of $5.02 in June 2022 to $2.95 according to AAA, they were not below $2.30 in most states; only one state, Oklahoma, had an average gas price around $2.30 as of Feb. 24, according to AAA data. The state with the nation's lowest average price on Feb. 24 was Oklahoma at $2.37 a gallon; Arkansas, Kansas and Mississippi were the other states with average prices at or below $2.50 a gallon, and another nine states had gasoline between $2.50 and $2.60 a gallon. Only eight gas stations nationwide were selling a gallon of gas for under $2, according to GasBuddy. Furthermore, by early March 2026, prices were rising rapidly: gasoline prices rose to the highest levels of President Trump's second term in March 2026, with AAA reporting a national average of about $3.32 per gallon, making the claim even more detached from reality by March 4.
