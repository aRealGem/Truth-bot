❌ False
Model reasoning — Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7

BLS data contradicts Trump's claim. Core CPI (all items less food and energy) was 2.6% for the 12 months ending December 2025 and 2.63% in November 2025. However, core CPI was substantially lower multiple times in the prior five years — for example, core CPI was around 1.3-1.6% in early 2021 (during the COVID-era low before the inflation surge). Therefore, a 2.6% core inflation rate in late 2025 is NOT the lowest in more than five years — it is actually higher than readings from 2020-early 2021. FactCheck.org also noted core inflation rose 3% from September 2024 to September 2025, and Fed Chair Powell said in December 2025 that 'inflation remains somewhat elevated.' The claim is not supported by BLS primary data.
