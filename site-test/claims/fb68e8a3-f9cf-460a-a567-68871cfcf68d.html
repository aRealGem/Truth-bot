The claim that the border is the "strongest and most secure in American history" is an exaggeration, though data from early 2026 shows a significant decrease in illegal crossings. In January 2026, U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) reported 34,626 total encounters nationwide, with 6,070 Border Patrol apprehensions on the southwest border, a 79% decrease from January 2025. Data for the first quarter of Fiscal Year 2026 (Oct-Dec 2025) showed the lowest number of southwest border apprehensions to start a fiscal year on record. DHS and CBP officials have characterized this as "the most secure border in American history." However, the phrase "strongest and most secure in American history" is difficult to prove definitively, as historical data collection methods vary and other metrics beyond apprehensions (like got-aways, drug seizures, and resourcing) contribute to overall security. While current enforcement has led to historically low encounter numbers, calling it the most secure ever is a subjective assessment.
