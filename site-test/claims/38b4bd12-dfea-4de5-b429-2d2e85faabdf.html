Data from multiple sources shows that mortgage rates in March 2022 were significantly lower than in March 2026, making the claim false. Around the claim's date, the average 30-year fixed mortgage rate was approximately 6.00%, according to a Freddie Mac survey for the week of March 5, 2026. In contrast, the average rate for March 2022 was 4.17%. Rates in early 2022 were even lower, with the January 2022 average at 3.22% before rising sharply throughout the year. While rates in early 2026 had decreased from highs of over 8% seen in late 2023, they had not returned to the lower levels of early 2022.
