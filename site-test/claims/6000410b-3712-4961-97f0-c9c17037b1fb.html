Trump's claim has two parts. The first part—that millions of illegal aliens crossed the border during the prior four years—is accurate: CBP recorded approximately 11 million border encounters over the course of Biden's four years. The second part—that the U.S. now has the strongest and most secure border in history—is strongly supported by CBP/DHS operational data, though 'most secure ever' is partly a rhetorical characterization. Tier 1 DHS data show nationwide encounters hit record lows: October FY2026 had 30,561 total encounters (29% below the prior October record of 43,010 in FY2012), and October–November FY2026 totaled 60,940, 28% below the previous record low of 84,293 in FY2012. USBP Q1 FY2026 apprehensions (21,815) were the lowest ever for a fiscal year's first quarter, 95% below the Biden Q1 average. March 2025 saw the lowest monthly border encounters in recorded history (~7,181 USBP apprehensions, 95% below March 2024). DHS also reports eight consecutive months of zero USBP releases as of January 2026. However, NPR and NYT note the claim of 'zero illegal aliens admitted' is exaggerated—Border Patrol still recorded just over 6,000 illegal crossings in January, and 'gotaways' continue at reduced levels. Thus the overarching 'strongest/most secure border' claim is directionally true and supported by record-low primary-source data, while some supporting rhetoric (e.g., zero crossings) is overstated.
