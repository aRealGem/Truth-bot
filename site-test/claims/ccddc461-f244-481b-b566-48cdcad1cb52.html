Trump's claim that gasoline is below $2.30 a gallon in most states as of March 4, 2026 is flatly contradicted by government and industry data. According to AAA on March 5, 2026 (one day after the claim), the national average for regular gasoline had jumped nearly 27 cents to $3.25 per gallon, and the cheapest state average was Oklahoma at $2.79 — meaning zero states, let alone 'most,' had averages below $2.30. The top 10 least expensive states all ranged from $2.79 to $2.92. EIA data confirms prices were rising sharply in early 2026 due to Middle East conflict, with the April 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook forecasting retail gasoline to average $3.70/gal for 2026. Independent fact-checkers (FactCheck.org, PolitiFact, KCCI) had already debunked this same claim, noting no state had an average below $2.60, and the lowest state average in mid-January was $2.34 in Oklahoma. LendingTree's AAA analysis shows the monthly average was $2.81 in January and $2.91 in February 2026 nationally — nowhere near $2.30 in most states.
