Trump's claim that Biden gave the U.S. the worst inflation in history is contradicted by BLS data. Trump did not inherit 'the worst inflation' in American history. The worst inflation occurred after World War I, when the largest 12-month price increase was 23.7% from June 1919 to June 1920. Also, from March 1979 to March 1980, overall inflation rose 14.8%. The peak inflation rate during Biden's term was 9.1% in June 2022, which was the highest 12-month increase since November 1981 — but the worst inflation in U.S. history was after World War I, when CPI was up 23.7% for the 12 months ending in June 1920. While Biden-era inflation was the worst in roughly 40 years, it was not the worst in U.S. history.
