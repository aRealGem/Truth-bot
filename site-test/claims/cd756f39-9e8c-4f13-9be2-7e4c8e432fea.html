BLS data show core CPI (all items less food and energy) rose 2.6% YoY in November 2025 and 2.6% YoY in December 2025 — not 1.7%. So 1.7% may be technically defensible as a Q4 2025 3-month-annualized SAAR figure, but it is far below the standard YoY measure of 2.6% that most listeners would understand as 'core inflation.' Additionally, October and November 2025 CPI data were affected by the federal government shutdown, complicating any clean Q4 measurement.
