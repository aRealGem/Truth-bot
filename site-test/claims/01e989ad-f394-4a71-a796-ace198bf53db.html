anthropic
📊 Exaggerated
Model reasoning — Anthropic Claude Opus 4.7

The claim is overstated. The Big Beautiful Bill, signed July 4, 2025, did not eliminate taxes on tips entirely — it created a temporary above-the-line federal income tax deduction for qualified tips, capped at $25,000 per year, available for tax years 2025-2028, per IRS guidance. The deduction phases out for taxpayers with modified adjusted gross income over $150,000 ($300,000 joint filers); it applies only to federal income tax (not payroll/FICA taxes); only workers in occupations 'customarily and regularly receiving tips' qualify; and many low-wage tipped workers already owe no federal income tax because of the standard deduction. CNBC notes that only 4 million workers (~2.5% of the workforce) hold tipped jobs. So while the bill provides meaningful tax relief on tips, it does not 'eliminate taxes on tips' broadly.
