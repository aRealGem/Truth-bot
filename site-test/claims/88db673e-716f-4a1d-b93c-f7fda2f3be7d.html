The White House announced in December 2025 that core inflation hit its lowest level in nearly five years, and Trading Economics confirmed core CPI at 2.5% in January and February 2026, described as the lowest since March 2021 — a roughly five-year low reached within Trump's first 12 months in office (inaugurated January 20, 2025).
