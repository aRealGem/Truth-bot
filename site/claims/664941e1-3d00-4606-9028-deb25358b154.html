Trump did sign an executive order on May 12, 2025 titled 'Delivering Most-Favored-Nation Prescription Drug Pricing to American Patients,' and his administration has secured voluntary MFN agreements with 17 major pharmaceutical manufacturers (covering about 86% of the branded drug market) including Pfizer, Eli Lilly, Novo Nordisk, Merck, and Regeneron. Under MFN, US prices are tied to the lowest price in OECD countries with at least 60% of US per capita GDP, applied to single-source brand drugs without generic competition. However, the claim that this 'will give Americans the lowest prescription drug prices in the world' is overstated — the policy targets parity with peer developed nations, not supremacy over all countries. Additionally, the agreements are largely voluntary, primarily affect Medicaid and a new TrumpRx direct-purchase channel, and many provisions have not yet been fully implemented. The 2020 version of this policy was struck down by courts.
