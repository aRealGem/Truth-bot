Trump did make this claim during his State of the Union address, but multiple fact-checkers (FactCheck.org, Poynter/PolitiFact, AP, NPR, BBC, DW, The Guardian) have found it significantly overstated. Several listed conflicts were not actually wars (the Egypt-Ethiopia dam dispute is a diplomatic disagreement with no agreement reached; the Serbia-Kosovo issue was not a war). Some 'ended' conflicts continue: the DRC-Rwanda violence persists despite the June 2025 agreement, and Cambodia-Thailand ceasefire violations resumed in December 2025 with new airstrikes. India has consistently denied US mediation in the Pakistan-India ceasefire, attributing it to direct DGMO-level military communications. Some agreements (like Serbia-Kosovo normalization) date to Trump's first term. The Gaza ceasefire and Israel-Iran truce are genuine accomplishments, but the overall 'eight wars ended' tally is widely characterized by independent fact-checkers as an overstatement.
