Grounding is only on synchronous/streaming calls today, so `supports_batch=False` is the correct setting, not a TODO. Current reality: Gemini participates in batch-mode consensus via the **live-sidecar path** (`GeminiAdapter.call()` runs live during submit with full grounding + context caching; verdicts spooled to `metrics/batch_sidecar/<run_id>.jsonl` and merged at reconcile). The only thing we'd gain from a real native batch is the ~50% discount Anthropic/OpenAI get — at the cost of losing grounding, which would tank verdict quality. Reopen when Google ships grounding in their batch tier (or when it's worth maintaining a separate Vertex + GCS credential chain for the price break). |
