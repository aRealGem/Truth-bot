Corrections

When a published verdict is found to be wrong — a reasoning error, evidence outside the claim's era, a misread referent — it is corrected publicly, per fact-checking norms: the claim keeps a visible correction note on its provenance strip, and every change is logged here with the old and new verdict, the reason, and the date. Corrections are never applied silently.
