Corrections

When a published verdict is found to be wrong — a reasoning error, evidence outside the claim's era, a misread referent — it is corrected publicly, per fact-checking norms: the claim keeps a visible correction note on its provenance strip, and every change is logged here with the old and new verdict, the reason, and the date. Corrections are never applied silently.

2026-07-21 — A systematic audit of panel reasoning (every claim decided by proposer-critic agreement without arbiter review) found a class of shared misreads: tenses, quarterly-vs-annual figures, baselines, and invented referents. 17 verdicts were revised with editor approval. As a result, the Donald Trump SOTU 2026 headline moved from 'Largely False' to 'Mostly False' (102 of 150 decided claims false-leaning); the Joe Biden SOTU 2022 headline remains 'Largely True' and strengthened.

No corrections have been issued for the currently published reports.
