Evidence shows deportations in FY2025 (442,637 per E3, or ~340,000 per E5) were elevated compared to prior years and represent a 10-year high (E6), supporting the 'record' framing. However, E2 reveals that claims about criminal records among deportees conflate legal and illegal immigrants, and E1 shows ICE prioritizes criminal convictions but does not establish that most deportees are criminals. The claim's rhetorical framing ('illegal alien criminals') suggests the deportations target primarily criminal aliens, but the evidence does not confirm that the bulk of those deported are criminals with convictions or pending charges—only that ICE prioritizes such cases. The 'record numbers' claim is accurate as of Feb 2026, but the implicit assertion that these are predominantly criminal deportations is unsupported and contradicted by E2's correction of similar misleading rhetoric.
