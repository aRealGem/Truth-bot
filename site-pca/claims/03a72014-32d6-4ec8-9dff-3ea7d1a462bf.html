Model reasoning — Hydramind-pca Pca

The core assertion—that modest additional contributions could grow accounts to over $100,000 by age 18—is mathematically possible under favorable conditions but is presented without material qualification. E1 confirms Trump made this exact claim. E2 shows that $1,000 alone (no additional contributions) grows to only $6,000 by age 18, implying the $100,000+ figure requires substantial additional contributions beyond 'modest' ones. E4 indicates that even with 'modest' market performance, balances reach ~$187,000 at 18, but this appears to assume meaningful ongoing contributions; the 'high growth' scenario reaching $700,000+ suggests the $100,000 claim is at the conservative end of projections. The claim overstates what 'modest' contributions realistically achieve by omitting that reaching $100,000+ requires either significant additional deposits or favorable market conditions, or both. The underlying claim is real but exaggerates the ease of reaching that threshold with truly modest contributions.
