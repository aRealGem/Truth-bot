The core assertion—that Republicans in Congress are investing a 'record number of dollars' in armed forces—is partially supported but exaggerated. FY2025 defense appropriations ($852.2B per E1, E2) represent only a 3.3% increase over FY2024 (E2), and FY2024 itself was higher than prior years, making FY2025 not a historical record but a modest increase. The claim of 'record' spending is misleading because it omits that defense spending has been at or near these levels for years; the increase is incremental, not unprecedented. The secondary claim about 'more people working today than ever before in the history of our country' cannot be verified from the provided evidence (E4 is unrelated), but the primary 'record dollars' assertion is the core and is contradicted by the modest percentage increase shown in the evidence.
