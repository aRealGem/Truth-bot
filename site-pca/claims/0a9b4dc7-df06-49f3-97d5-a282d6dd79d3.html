The core assertion is that the murder decline was 'the biggest decline...in recorded history.' The evidence shows the 2023 decline (11.6%) was the largest *single-year* decline on record *since 1960* (E5) or in the modern FBI dataset, but the claim's absolute language ('recorded history') overstates this. E4 notes the murder rate is 'lower than it was during the 1970s,' and E5 specifies data goes 'back to 1960,' meaning the claim lacks support for the full span of recorded history. The decline is real and historically large within available FBI records, but the absolute framing exceeds what the evidence supports—it was the biggest recent decline, not necessarily in all of recorded history.
