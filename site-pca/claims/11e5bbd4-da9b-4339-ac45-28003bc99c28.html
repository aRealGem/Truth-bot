Model reasoning — Hydramind-pca Pca

The core assertion—that sanctions prevented Russia's central bank from defending the ruble and made the $630B war fund worthless—is partially true but materially overstated. Evidence confirms (1) sanctions did restrict CBR's ability to support the ruble (E1, E3, E5), (2) the $630B reserve was frozen/inaccessible (E2, E4), and (3) the ruble lost significant value immediately (E3, E6). However, 'worthless' is an absolute claim contradicted by the fact that Russia's central bank later successfully stabilized the ruble through capital controls and other measures not mentioned here; the frozen reserves, while inaccessible to international markets, retained intrinsic value and were not rendered worthless. The claim cherry-picks the immediate crisis moment (late Feb–early Mar 2022) without acknowledging that Russia's CBR subsequently managed currency defense through alternative means, making the 'worthless' characterization an exaggeration of a real but incomplete outcome.
