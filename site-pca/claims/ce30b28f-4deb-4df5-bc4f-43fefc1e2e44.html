The core assertion is that a prior government shutdown cost 'two points on G.D.P.' The evidence shows the actual GDP impact was approximately 1 percentage point (E1: 'subtracted about 1 percentage point'), not 2 points. E2 and E3 provide context that weekly impacts are 0.1–0.2 ppts, and a full-quarter shutdown would reduce growth by 1.2–2.4 ppts; the actual measured impact was ~1 ppt. The claim exaggerates the documented economic damage by roughly double, making it a material overstatement of a real event.
