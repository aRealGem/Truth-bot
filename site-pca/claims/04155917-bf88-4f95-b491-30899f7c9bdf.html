The core assertion conflates two distinct claims: (1) that roadside bombs killed/maimed thousands of American service members, and (2) that they killed 'even millions of people' overall. Evidence supports the first: E1 documents 1,790 U.S. deaths from IEDs in Iraq (2011–2020) and 828 in Afghanistan; E2 adds 539 in Afghanistan over 9 years; E3 and E5 confirm IEDs were a major cause of U.S. casualties. However, the claim that IEDs killed 'millions of people' is unsupported and implausible—no evidence provided quantifies total civilian or combatant deaths from roadside bombs globally or in Iraq/Afghanistan at that scale. The characterization of 'they' (Iran/Iranian proxies) as 'kings of the roadside bomb' is partially supported (E5 mentions Iranian-made explosively formed penetrators killed 196 U.S. soldiers), but the 'millions' figure is an extreme exaggeration unsupported by evidence, making the overall claim misleading despite the accurate core about thousands of American casualties.
