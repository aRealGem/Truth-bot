The core assertion—that Beckstrom died *in order to defend* the capital—implies intentional sacrifice in combat or defense of the Capitol building itself. Evidence shows she was an Army National Guard specialist who died from injuries sustained during a 'presence patrol' on a street corner in D.C., apparently shot by another service member (Andrew Wolfe), not in direct defense of the Capitol or in combat. E1 and E6 use similar patriotic framing ('defending the capital,' 'defend our capital'), but E5 clarifies she was on a routine 'presence patrol' to deter local crime, not engaged in active defense. The claim romanticizes her death as purposeful defense when the evidence indicates she was killed in a tragic incident during a patrol, not while actively defending the Capitol. This is a real death of a service member in the nation's capital, but the characterization of *why* she died—'in order to defend'—misrepresents the actual circumstances.
