The core assertion is that the typical 401(k) balance increased by at least $30,000 since taking office. E5 (CNBC, 2026-02-26, near utterance date) reports the average 401(k) balance hit a record $148,153 across all age groups as of early 2026. E6 (PSCA, 2025-11-21) reports the average balance increased 9.1% year-over-year to $144,400 from Q3 2024. A 9.1% increase on ~$132,500 (prior year balance) yields approximately $12,000, not $30,000. Even accounting for market gains and contributions, the evidence shows growth of roughly $12,000–$15,000 year-over-year, substantially below the claimed $30,000. The claim exaggerates the actual increase by roughly double, making it a material overstatement of a real but much smaller gain.
