The core assertion—that the Biden administration (2021–2025, 'four long years') secured less than $1 trillion in new investment—is contradicted by Treasury data (E5) showing $430 billion in *excess* business investment above historical trends since 2019, and by the baseline U.S. gross private domestic investment of ~$5.4 trillion annually (E6), which would total ~$21.6 trillion over four years. The claim conflates 'new investment announcements' or 'commitments' (which Trump's own statements cite at inflated levels per E1, E3) with actual realized investment. The underlying assertion that Biden-era investment was lower than Trump's is real but unsubstantiated by the evidence; however, the absolute claim of 'less than $1 trillion' in total U.S. business investment over four years is demonstrably exaggerated and misleading, as actual domestic investment vastly exceeded that figure. The claim cherry-picks a narrow metric (possibly foreign announcements or specific sectors) while ignoring the broader, documented investment reality.
