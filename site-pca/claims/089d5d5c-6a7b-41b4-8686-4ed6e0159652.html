The core assertion is that the administration cut a record number of job-killing regulations. Evidence shows 646 deregulatory actions were completed (E5) and a record number of Biden-era regulations were overturned (E6), supporting a substantial deregulatory effort. However, the claim uses the absolute qualifier 'record number' without temporal specificity. E5 documents 646 completed actions in 2025, but does not establish this as a historical record compared to prior administrations' deregulatory output. E6 specifies the record was for overturning Biden regulations specifically, not regulations generally. The characterization of all targeted regulations as 'job-killing' is rhetorical assertion unsupported by evidence. The 10-to-1 executive order (E2) was signed but evidence does not confirm it was fully implemented or that the net result exceeded prior deregulatory records. The claim overstates the evidentiary support by asserting an unqualified 'record' when evidence shows substantial deregulation but does not definitively establish it as the highest in history.
