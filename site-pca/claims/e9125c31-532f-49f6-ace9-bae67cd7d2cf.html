Model reasoning — Hydramind-pca Pca

The core assertion has two parts: (1) millions of illegal aliens poured across borders unvetted during the prior four years, and (2) the U.S. now has the strongest and most secure border in American history. Part 1 is supported: E1 and E2 confirm 2.5+ million encounters under Biden, with 2022 reaching an all-time high of 2.2 million. Part 2 is partially supported by recent data: E4, E5, and E6 show dramatic reductions in 2025–2026 apprehensions (down 90% from July 2024, lowest monthly totals in CBP history, 96% decrease from Biden average). However, the claim's absolute assertion—'strongest and most secure border in American history by far'—is exaggerated. E2 notes the 2022 peak was an all-time high in encounters, meaning prior years had lower numbers; the current low is a return to historical norms, not an unprecedented achievement. The claim conflates a sharp recent improvement with an absolute historical superlative. The underlying trend (massive prior inflows, now sharply reduced) is real, but the framing as 'strongest...in American history by far' overstates the evidence, which shows a return to lower historical baselines rather than an unprecedented security level.
