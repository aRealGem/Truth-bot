The core assertion—that America 'used to have' the best roads, bridges, and airports—is presented as historical fact, but the evidence shows America's infrastructure advantage was never absolute or universally acknowledged. E2 and E3 indicate that by 2011, America was already 'lagging behind most of the rest of the industrialized world' in infrastructure investment, and over 30 years prior (1980s) road/bridge capacity had grown only 7% despite doubled vehicle miles. This contradicts the implication that America held clear global superiority in infrastructure until recently. The claim cherry-picks a nostalgic past without acknowledging that infrastructure decline was gradual and America's relative standing had eroded well before 2022. E4 confirms the claim was made on 2022-03-02, but the historical premise—that America definitively 'used to have' the best—overstates what the evidence supports.
