E2 (dated 2022-01-26, just before the utterance date of 2022-03-01) confirms the U.S. had sent 400 million doses to 112 nations by late January 2022. The claim that the U.S. sent 'more than any other nation' is contradicted or at minimum highly questionable given China's documented export volumes. The 475 million figure lacks contemporaneous verification, and the comparative claim ('more than any other nation') is not supported by the evidence.
