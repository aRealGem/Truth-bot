Model reasoning — Hydramind-pca Pca

The core assertion—that a drug costing $4,000 was obtained for under $500, representing 'a reduction of much more actually than $3,500'—contains a mathematically impossible claim. A reduction from $4,000 to under $500 is a price decrease of roughly 87.5% maximum, not a reduction 'of much more than $3,500' in the sense of percentage reduction. The phrase 'reduction of much more than $3,500' appears to conflate absolute dollar savings ($3,500+) with percentage reduction language, creating a false impression. Multiple fact-checkers (E2, E3, E4, E5) document that Trump administration officials have made mathematically impossible claims about drug price reductions exceeding 100%, which is impossible—a 100% reduction means free; anything beyond that would require negative prices. While the underlying claim that some drugs became cheaper through TrumpRx may be real, the framing here—especially 'a reduction of much more actually than $3,500'—distorts the math to exaggerate the savings in a way that mirrors the documented pattern of impossible percentage claims.
