Evidence shows seven Russian banks were removed from SWIFT (E2, E4) and Sberbank and Alfa Bank were sanctioned (E3), but 'largest banks' is imprecise: VTB (Russia's second-largest per E2) was removed, but the claim's absolute framing ('Russia's largest banks') suggests comprehensive coverage of the top tier. The underlying fact—major Russian banks were being disconnected from SWIFT and the international system—is real and accurate as of the utterance date, but the phrasing overstates the completeness and immediacy of the action. The claim is not false (the core event happened), but it exaggerates the scope and presents a snapshot of announced sanctions as if they were already fully implemented.
