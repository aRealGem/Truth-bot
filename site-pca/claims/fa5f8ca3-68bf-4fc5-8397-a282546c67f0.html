The core assertion—that Biden secured 'much less than $1 trillion' in four years while Trump brought in 'much more than $18 trillion' in one year—is factually stated by Trump and reported by fact-checkers (E1, E2). However, fact-checkers have flagged these figures as highly misleading. E2 and E3 indicate that Trump's $18–22 trillion claims conflate announced/pledged corporate investments with actual federal spending or investment, and Bloomberg Economics reported these figures are 'short trillions' of the claimed amount. The comparison itself cherry-picks different metrics (Biden's actual new federal investments vs. Trump's announced private-sector pledges) and strips context about what constitutes a genuine 'investment.' While the numbers as stated by Trump are documented, the underlying claim exaggerates the real difference by mixing incomparable categories and overstating Trump's figures.
